Common Questions

Everything you need to know

If you cannot find the information you need, feel free to contact our team .

What services does PawCare provide?

PawCare provides a range of pet care and wellness services designed to support the everyday health and wellbeing of pets. Visit our services page to explore the available care options.

How do I book an appointment?

You can request an appointment through our online appointment form . Provide your contact details, your pet's information, preferred date and time, and the service you are interested in.

Can I choose my preferred appointment time?

Yes. Our appointment form allows you to provide your preferred date and time. Your request is then reviewed by the PawCare team before the appointment is confirmed.

Do you care for both dogs and cats?

PawCare is designed to support the care needs of common household pets, including dogs and cats. When requesting an appointment, select your pet type so our team can better understand your needs.

What should I bring to an appointment?

Bring any relevant information about your pet's health or previous care that may help our team understand their needs. If you are unsure what to bring, you can contact PawCare before your appointment.

Can I contact PawCare before booking?

Absolutely. If you have questions about our services or are unsure which type of care is appropriate for your pet, use our contact page to send us an enquiry.

Where can I learn more about the PawCare team?

You can visit our team page to learn more about the professionals behind PawCare and our approach to compassionate pet care.

How can I stay informed about pet wellness?

Visit the PawCare blog for practical articles and educational information about pet care, wellness, nutrition, and everyday routines.
