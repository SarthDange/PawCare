# PawCare — Project Plan

## 1. Project Purpose
Create a complete but manageable website for **PawCare Pet Care & Wellness Center**, a fictional local pet-care business.

The same business foundation will be reused across the Digital Marketing & Social Media course assignments instead of creating a new unrelated project for every assignment.

## 2. Business Concept
PawCare provides practical pet-care services for pet owners, with a focus on trustworthy care, convenience and pet wellbeing.

The website should feel like a genuine local business rather than a college demonstration or AI-generated landing page.

## 3. Website Goals
1. Clearly explain PawCare's services.
2. Build trust through business information and team details.
3. Allow visitors to contact PawCare.
4. Allow visitors to request appointments.
5. Provide useful pet-care content.
6. Establish a strong SEO foundation.
7. Provide enough data and functionality for later marketing/analytics assignments.

## 4. Public Pages
### Home
Business introduction, major services, trust points, useful content preview and clear appointment/contact CTA.

### About
PawCare story, values, approach to pet care and business information.

### Services
Detailed service offerings and useful service information.

### Team
Staff/caregiver profiles and roles.

### Blog / Pet Care Tips
Useful, realistic articles that can later support content marketing and SEO work.

### FAQ
Common customer questions about services, appointments and general processes.

### Contact
Business contact information plus enquiry form.

### Appointment
Appointment-request form connected to the backend/database.

## 5. Functional Features
### Visitor
- Navigate all public pages.
- View services.
- View team.
- Read pet-care content.
- Submit contact enquiry.
- Submit appointment request.

### Admin
A small administrative area may be included for:
- viewing appointment requests
- updating appointment status
- viewing contact enquiries

Admin functionality should remain simple and purposeful.

## 6. Course Assignment Reuse
The website should provide a foundation for:
- Website creation and hosting
- SEO audit
- Keyword research and on-page SEO
- Search advertising simulation
- Social media strategy
- Content calendar
- Meta advertising simulation
- Email/content marketing
- Blog/SEO content
- Analytics and conversion tracking
- Case-study work
- Complete digital marketing strategy

The website itself does not need every future assignment feature implemented immediately. It needs a solid foundation that can support them.

## 7. SEO Foundation
Public pages should be created with:
- unique page titles
- meta descriptions
- logical H1/H2/H3 structure
- descriptive URLs
- natural keyword usage
- image alt text
- internal links
- relevant external links where appropriate
- mobile-friendly layout
- optimized images
- useful CTAs

Technical SEO will include, where appropriate:
- `sitemap.xml`
- `robots.txt`
- canonical URLs
- favicon
- Open Graph metadata
- structured data where appropriate

## 8. Scope Control
Do not add:
- online payment systems
- complex customer portals
- real-time appointment availability
- unnecessary AI features
- large e-commerce functionality
- real-time chat
- complex dashboards unless needed for the course

## 9. Definition of Done
PawCare is considered ready when:
- the major public pages work
- shared components work
- forms submit correctly
- database storage works
- backend routes work
- responsive design works
- basic security practices are applied
- SEO basics are implemented
- the website looks like a realistic business website
- it can be hosted and demonstrated
